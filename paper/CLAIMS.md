## One testable main claim

**SymQ is a specialized exact inspection and verification engine for reversible
Boolean circuits: its native monomial-ZDD implementation can share symbolic ANFs
and replay distinguishing inputs, but whether it is useful relative to explicit
ANF, BDD, PolyBoRi or SAT depends on the requested service, circuit family, variable
order and resource budget.**

This is an engineering claim, not a new representation, algebraic decision
procedure, complexity bound or assertion of universal superiority. The practical
user is a compiler engineer who needs exact intermediate Boolean functions or a
checked local/full functional-equivalence query. A user who needs only a verdict
need not pay to enumerate ANF monomials.

## Historical results (exploratory, not current-head claims)

The October 27 campaigns used native binary SHA-256
`2e3f67910a34cfc0c12c855840ec83ff7459a3f10b12d5572f3ef3b18e203f30`, from
sources predating the count-overflow and width-64 fixes and later ABI changes.
They remain useful as exploratory evidence with known limitations:

- The old minimal native loop beat PolyBoRi on common completed examples; the
  apparent advantage over dynamic CUDD did not generalize across the old five
  generated families.
- The old fused loop gained after the harness's input-construction boundary was
  corrected; factor sorting was adverse on that corpus. These are different
  observations, not mutually exclusive claims.
- Multiplier/random instances and unlucky variable orders produced representation
  growth, timeouts, kills and unrun cells. Those are retained in the data.
- The old corpus formerly called blind is now explored. It cannot be reused as an
  untouched holdout. Old bootstrap intervals over circuit variants do not supply
  independent cross-family observations.
- A binding calibration on cache-hit operands does not bound operand-dependent
  calls and cannot prove which mechanism caused an observed ratio.

Exact directories and deviations are retained in `EXPERIMENTS.md` and
`REPRODUCIBILITY.md`, explicitly labeled historical. Current qualification tables
must cite their own source commit, binary hash, manifest hash and complete status
counts. No macro derived from the old directories is a current performance result.

The current manifest's blanket `new_holdout` label also needs a case-level
adjudication: increment ignores its seed, and the small deterministic instance
was already generated by development tests. It is calibration, not an untouched
holdout. The raw/source record is preserved; the original aggregate is a
descriptive mixed study, with separately labeled holdout-only sensitivity rather
than replacement or rerunning of cases. Two small holdout families do not
establish population-level generalization.

## Explicit non-contributions and promotion gates

No first ANF/ZDD representation; no new GF(2) product recurrence; no first HMR
semantics; no universal quantum verifier; no asymptotic speedup; no proof that
native is generally faster; no machine-checked implementation or independent
UNSAT certificate. A replayed witness is evidence for a difference, not a
certificate for an equivalence result.

The installed sign/HMR, reduced-channel, coherent-domain, instrument, dyadic and
cyclotomic research engines are real implementations with separate contracts,
not unfinished copies to recreate. Their existence does not expand the theorem
of this classical paper. They remain experimental unless separately qualified.
